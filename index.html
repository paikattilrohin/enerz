About us

Founded in 2024, EnerZ is an Indian sustainable e-mobility pioneer building a fully integrated electric ecosystem spanning commercial fleets, advanced charging infrastructure, and a unified digital platform. What began as an electric two-wheeler delivery fleet has rapidly evolved into a multi-state operation leasing EV cars, commercial trucks, and intercity electric buses.

Proof, not promises

Running electric fleets for India’s biggest delivery platforms since 2024.

Clean power, not just clean vehicles

Hubs designed to generate solar energy with battery storage and micro-grids.

One app for the whole journey

Uniting ride-hailing, charging maps, delivery logistics, and payments in a single platform.

Three businesses One ecosystem

The fleet creates demand for the charging network, the network lowers the fleet’s running cost, and the platform ties bookings, operations and payments together.
